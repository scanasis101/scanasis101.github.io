Our Story

Built on Precision, Driven by Quality

Scanasis specialises in converting real-world conditions into intelligent digital models using structured BIM workflows and quality-controlled delivery — from measured building surveys to full digital twin creation.

Founded by digital engineering professionals, we combine deep technical expertise with a relentless focus on accuracy. Every project is handled by trained BIM modellers working within rigorous QA protocols to ensure deliverables meet international standards.

We partner with architecture and engineering firms, contractors, facility managers, and heritage organisations globally — delivering scan-to-BIM, point cloud processing, and VDC services that integrate seamlessly into your project workflow.

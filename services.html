How It Works

Our Proven Five-Step
Delivery Process

Every project follows a structured, quality-controlled workflow — from raw scan ingestion to verified final deliverables.

01

Receive Survey Data

We securely receive your raw scan files — E57, RCP/RCS, PTS, LAS — via encrypted upload. Our team validates data completeness and coverage before proceeding.

  • E57 • RCP / RCS
  • PTS • LAS / LAZ
  • Leica • FARO • Trimble
02

Process Point Clouds

Raw scans are registered, merged, denoised, and classified. We produce clean, accurate point cloud datasets ready for BIM extraction with defined coordinate systems.

  • Registration • Alignment
  • Noise Filtering • Classification
  • Coordinate System Setup
03

Develop BIM Models

Our certified BIM engineers model architectural, structural, and MEP elements against the point cloud reference to the specified LOD with full parametric data.

  • LOD 200 → LOD 500
  • Architectural • Structural • MEP
  • Revit • ArchiCAD • IFC
04

Quality Assurance

Every model undergoes a rigorous multi-stage QA review — dimensional accuracy checks, clash validation, LOD compliance, and client-specific standard verification.

  • Dimensional Accuracy Check
  • LOD Compliance Review
  • Client Standard Verification
05

Final Delivery

Approved deliverables are packaged and securely transferred in your required formats with full documentation, revision notes, and ongoing support available.

  • RVT • IFC • DWG • PDF
  • Encrypted Secure Transfer
  • Post-Delivery Support
